Request deletion

To request deletion of your Hangarin account and associated personal data, contact the Hangarin administrator with the email address used for your account. Include the subject Delete my Hangarin data.

What happens next

We will verify that the request belongs to you, delete or anonymize account information and social sign-in associations where applicable, and confirm when the request is complete.

Meta callback

This page is also available as a public callback URL for social sign-in providers. A POST request receives a JSON confirmation response, while deletion requests are reviewed to prevent unauthorized account deletion.

Need help?

Use the account email associated with Hangarin when submitting a deletion request so we can verify and process it.
